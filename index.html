Frequently asked questions

Important details about the current version.

Are all five plans available for purchase?

No. The five plans are displayed as proposed pricing. Payment processing has not been implemented.

Does image or video generation work yet?

This version prepares request details and calculates estimated credits. A real AI provider still needs to be connected.

Are Normal and Better free?

Both have zero additional model credits in this interface. The eventual cost of running an actual AI model must still be checked.

How are video credits calculated?

Duration costs 50, 60, 80 or 100 credits. High adds 10 and Ultra adds 20. The displayed total combines duration and model costs.

Will credits be deducted when I click a button?

No. The current page only calculates estimated costs. Actual balances and deductions require a secure backend.

Will you add Nano Banana and Veo?

Provider-specific names should only be shown when those models are genuinely integrated. Nano Banana belongs in an image-generation/editing context, not as a generic video model.
