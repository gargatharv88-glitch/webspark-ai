Frequently asked questions

What to know before using WebSpark AI.

Are image and video generation working right now?

Not yet. This version lets you choose options and calculate estimated credits. A real AI model and secure backend still need to be connected.

Are the first two quality levels free?

The first two tiers add zero model credits in this interface. This does not guarantee that an eventual generation provider will be free to operate.

How are video credits calculated?

The duration costs 50, 60, 80, or 100 credits. High adds 10 model credits and Ultra adds 20 model credits. The displayed total is duration plus model cost.

Will credits actually be deducted?

No. These are estimates only. Real deductions need a secure server-side credit system so visitors cannot change their balance in the browser.

Why aren't the models called Veo or Nano Banana?

Provider-specific names should only be displayed when those models are genuinely integrated. Nano Banana is associated with image generation/editing, not a generic video model. This version uses quality tiers to avoid claiming unsupported integrations.
