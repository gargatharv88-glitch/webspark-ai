Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

Is WebSpark AI free to use?

The current website interface is free to explore. Actual AI generation will be enabled after a compatible free model or service is configured.

Can I generate real AI videos right now?

Not yet. The Image Animator interface is prepared, but a working video-generation engine still needs to be connected and tested.

Are the subscription plans active?

No. The prices and credit amounts are proposed plans only. Payment functionality has not been activated.

Will the website work on mobile?

The page uses a responsive layout intended for mobile, tablet and desktop browsers.

Will my uploaded images be stored?

This frontend does not upload images to a server. A future backend must clearly explain its image storage and privacy behavior before generation is enabled.
